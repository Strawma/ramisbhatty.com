## What the module covered

This module covered requirements gathering and software modelling, using UML to describe a system's structure and behaviour and Event-B to express its state, constraints, and transitions formally.

Our group coursework modelled an NHS dental appointment system. I contributed to the UML activity diagram and produced the state-machine diagram. For the subsequent Event-B submission, I handled the LaTeX report and merged the group's work into a combined model and document.

## What I took from it

UML-style diagrams have been especially useful for planning and documenting my projects. Even without following every notation rule, they help me explain how a system fits together and break an ambitious project into smaller, manageable components.
