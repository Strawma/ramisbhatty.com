## What the module covered

This module taught us the importance of proper software modelling and design, and how to use formal methods to ensure that our designs are correct. We learned about different modelling techniques, such as UML and Event-B, and how to apply them to real-world problems. The module also covered requirements gathering and how to translate user needs into formal specifications.

## What I took from it

UML diagrams have been indespensable for me; while I'm not too fussed on absolute correctness, I find UML-like diagrams to be a very useful tool for planning and documenting the flow of my projects. They are a nice abstraction that let me break down ambitious projects into smaller, more manageable components.
