## What the module covered

This module taught us how to properly research and present information in a professional context. Assessment covered both an independent literature review as well as a group presentation.

## What I took from it

My third year project and general documentation for my projects has been greatly influenced by what was taught to me in this module. Dedicating time to properly research and document my work has helped me produce higher quality work that others can understand.
