## What the module covered

This module covered a number of mathematical concepts that are foundational to computer science. This included topics such as set theory, graphy theory, counting and statistics and logic. 

## What I took from it

While this is one of those "boring theory modules" that doesn't have a direct application to programming, I found it very intellectually stimulating and it has helped me develop my problem solving and logical reasoning skills. I also personally found the set theory concepts (such as Cantor's diagonal argument for infinite sets) very interesting.
